# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can work on it without being harassed or belittled.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not the person.
- Be concrete: say what you ran, what you saw and what you expected.
- Respect other people's time and privacy; do not post anything private about anyone.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block contributors who do not follow this. To report a problem, use a private channel: GitHub's private contact through the repository's Security tab, marked as a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, which applies to the points above where this text is silent: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
